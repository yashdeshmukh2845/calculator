# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple script to calculate the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and the time period in years.

## Formula
$$\text{Simple Interest} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
- **P** = Principal Amount
- **R** = Annual Rate of Interest (%)
- **T** = Time Period (Years)

## Input Parameters
1. **Principal Amount**: The initial amount of money borrowed or invested.
2. **Rate of Interest**: The annual percentage rate charged or earned.
3. **Time**: The duration for which the money is borrowed/invested in years.

## Output
- **Simple Interest**: The calculated interest amount.
- **Total Amount**: Principal + Simple Interest.
